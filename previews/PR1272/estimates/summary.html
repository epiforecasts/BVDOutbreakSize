Headline estimates ​

  • Cumulative infections: the outbreak is estimated to have caused 30% 22351–26658, 60% 20437–29557, 90% 17279–36168 infections to date, reported and unreported.

  • Against the 11680 laboratory-confirmed cases by the cut-off that is roughly 1.5–3.1× as many infections, so confirmed cases are estimated to capture only a small share of the outbreak.

  • Outbreak start and age: the outbreak is estimated to have begun on a start date of 30% 2026-03-15–2026-03-21, 60% 2026-03-11–2026-03-24, 90% 2026-03-03–2026-03-27, an elapsed age to the cut-off of 30% 242–248, 60% 239–252, 90% 236–260 days.

  • Growth rate and doubling time: the initial growth rate is estimated to have been 30% 0.055–0.067, 60% 0.049–0.075, 90% 0.04–0.091 per day, an initial doubling time of 30% 12.6–10.4, 60% 14.0–9.2, 90% 17.1–7.7 days. The latest growth rate is estimated to be 30% -0.0–0.009, 60% -0.006–0.017, 90% -0.017–0.033 per day, a latest doubling time of 30% -1530.4–74.6, 60% -110.7–40.8, 90% -41.4–21.2 days.

  • Reproduction number: the initial reproduction number is estimated to have been 30% 1.93–2.21, 60% 1.81–2.41, 90% 1.64–2.87 and the latest to be 30% 0.99–1.13, 60% 0.91–1.25, 90% 0.79–1.5.

  • Case-fatality ratio: the case-fatality ratio is estimated to be 30% 0.49–0.54, 60% 0.46–0.57, 90% 0.4–0.63.

  • Shift from priors: how far the data has moved each estimate from its prior, in prior interquartile ranges, where a value of one means the posterior median sits one prior interquartile range from the prior median, zero means unchanged, and the sign gives the direction. The fit moves the cumulative infection count by -0.17, the outbreak age by -1.26 and the doubling time by 0.88; the largest move is in the outbreak age.

Health-zone forecast ​

Projected counts are for the week to 2026-11-25.

  • Most new confirmed cases: Beni about 94 (90% credible interval 44 to 179); Mandima about 57 (90% credible interval 20 to 126); Bunia about 54 (90% credible interval 19 to 116).

  • Chance of a case: 31 of 67 zones have at least an even chance of reporting a confirmed case, and 13 of reporting at least ten.

  • Quiet zones: 24 zones have had no case allocated over the past two weeks. Bambu is the most likely of them to report one, at 40%.
