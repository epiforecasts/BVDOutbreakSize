Province estimates ​

This page gives the province estimates from the joint model, whose methods are on the Methods page. The national estimates are the sum of the provinces here. The per-province forecast is on the province forecasts page and its scoring is in the forecast by province.

This page is generated from docs/pages/estimates/province.jl. The model code it calls is in src/. See aim and origins and limitations.

The provinces are coupled by the gravity kernel in the mixing and importation Methods section. The distances between the patches' population centres run from 191 km between Sud-Ubangi and Nord-Ubangi to 1 220 km between Sud-Kivu and Sud-Ubangi. Ituri is 324 km from Nord-Kivu and 216 km from Haut-Uele. About half of what leaves Nord-Kivu lands in Sud-Kivu, 260 km away and the second most populous of the eight. Nord-Ubangi sends about a third of its exports to Sud-Ubangi, its nearest neighbour.

Point prevalence ​

Point prevalence is the number of people infected at the cut-off who are still in the community, having not yet died, recovered or been detected as a suspected case. It splits into people not yet symptomatic and people with symptoms not yet detected, as the point prevalence Methods section sets out. A province's undetected count depends on its suspected-case detection probability. The province compositions see that probability only as a product with case confirmation, so the province occupancy splits and its prior set it. The table gives each rate per 100,000 residents and the number of people in the community, as medians with 90% credible intervals.

Province parameters against their priors ​

The pair plots below set the province parameters against their prior, drawn from the province model. The spatial hyperparameters against their prior are on the province in-sample checks page.

Draw from the patch model's prior
julia
prior_patch_chn = patch_prior_draws(obs);

Each province's pair plot sets the reproduction number at the cut-off against the relative case ascertainment, the case-fatality ratio against the relative death confirmation, and the importation intensity against the export weight relative to Ituri. Each pair is identified only as a product, so a ridge between the two is expected and the prior sets the position along it.

Data currency ​

The province blocks are the provincial tables of the situation reports: confirmed cases, confirmed deaths and the 24-hour laboratory volumes. A block that stops updating shows its last date before the cut-off.

Province block currency
julia
# Every province block whose last vintage falls before the cut-off, from
# the shared stream registry rather than a per-page list of dates. The
# grace the national table allows is not applied here: one vintage behind
# is already worth reading.
province_currency = let
    status = stream_report_status(obs; stratum = :province)
    behind = status[[ismissing(d) || d > 0 for d in status.days_since], :]
    isempty(behind) ?
        Markdown.parse(
            "Every province block reports to the cut-off, $(obs.cutoff)."
        ) :
        MarkdownTable(
            DataFrame(
                "Block" => behind.label,
                "Last reported" => [
                    ismissing(d) ? "never" : string(d)
                    for d in behind.last_date
                ],
                "Days before cut-off" => [
                    ismissing(d) ? "-" : string(d)
                    for d in behind.days_since
                ]
            )
        )
end;

Province fit diagnostics ​

The national fit diagnostics summarise the same joint fit as a whole, and its breakdown by parameter covers every parameter.

julia
# The quantities the sections above hold one per province: the
# reproduction number and infections to date from the renewal equation,
# the case-fatality ratio and the two ascertainment ratios the province
# compositions identify, and the importation intensity and export weight
# the per-province pair plots show.
province_diagnostic_quantities = [
    :R_T_patch, :C_T_patch, :CFR_patch, :province_ascertainment,
    :province_death_ascertainment, :importation_epsilon_patch, :export_weight,
]
province_diagnostics_labels = merge(
    province_pair_labels, Dict(:C_T_patch => "Infections to date")
)
province_diagnostics_frame = let df = parameter_diagnostics(chn_joint)
    df[in.(Symbol.(df.parameter), Ref(province_diagnostic_quantities)), :]
end
province_diagnostics_table = MarkdownTable(
    family_diagnostics_table(
        province_diagnostics_frame;
        n = length(province_diagnostic_quantities),
        labels = province_diagnostics_labels
    )
);

Saving province assets ​

The summary dashboard shows the province summary table, the province map, the reproduction number by province and the weekly cases against the reproduction number, so they are written here.

dashboard_dir = joinpath(
    pkgdir(BVDOutbreakSize), "docs", "src", "summary_assets"

# The summary's compact province table: infections to date, R at the
# cut-off and the confirmed cases over the latest week of reports, the
# same week the cases against R figure ends on.
province_recent_cases = let last_day = min(last(province_cases.days), obs.n)
    weekly_count_table(
        province_cases.days, province_cases.increments[1:N_PATCHES, :],
        PROVINCE_LABELS[1:N_PATCHES];
        cutoff = obs.cutoff - Day(obs.n - last_day), n = last_day, weeks = 1
    ).count
end

            chn_joint, N_PATCHES; recent_cases = province_recent_cases

end
