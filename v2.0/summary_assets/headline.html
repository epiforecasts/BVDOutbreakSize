On this page

  • Cumulative infections: the outbreak is estimated to have caused 30% 12646–14922, 60% 11430–15394, 90% 9947–18687 infections to date, reported and unreported.

  • Against the 7258 laboratory-confirmed cases by the cut-off that is roughly 1.4–2.6× as many infections, so confirmed cases are estimated to capture only a small share of the outbreak.

  • Outbreak start and age: the outbreak is estimated to have begun on a start date of 30% 2026-03-18–2026-03-20, 60% 2026-03-15–2026-03-21, 90% 2026-03-10–2026-03-24, an elapsed age to the cut-off of 30% 177–179, 60% 176–182, 90% 173–187 days.

  • Growth rate and doubling time: the initial growth rate is estimated to have been 30% 0.044–0.058, 60% 0.044–0.067, 90% 0.037–0.082 per day, an initial doubling time of 30% 15.6–12.0, 60% 15.7–10.4, 90% 18.7–8.5 days. The latest growth rate is estimated to be 30% 0.016–0.03, 60% 0.005–0.035, 90% -0.013–0.057 per day, a latest doubling time of 30% 43.6–23.1, 60% 144.1–19.8, 90% -53.1–12.2 days.

  • Reproduction number: the initial reproduction number is estimated to have been 30% 1.18–1.28, 60% 1.14–1.36, 90% 1.14–1.54 and the latest to be 30% 1.08–1.1, 60% 1.02–1.16, 90% 0.94–1.31.

  • Case-fatality ratio: the case-fatality ratio is estimated to be 30% 0.44–0.48, 60% 0.43–0.51, 90% 0.37–0.56.
